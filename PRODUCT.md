# StudAb27

## Register

product

## Users

 Indonesian secondary-school students preparing undergraduate engineering applications. They use the app between schoolwork and test preparation, often in the evening, and need a small next step rather than another long list.

The primary device is a phone, often used one-handed in a quiet room at night. Keep navigation at the bottom, forms readable, and conversation drafts safe when a tab is interrupted. Desktop is a comfortable extension of the same workspace.

## Product Purpose

Turn scholarship research into a sustainable application workflow. Schola tracks official-source changes, records evidence and uncertainty, compares funding against a family budget, and offers at most three short tasks. The catalogue supports deliberate research without taking over the home screen. One Schola corner holds conversation and bounded source reviews; no external chat channel is required.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, focused, reassuring. The user prefers a dark interface with the warmth and restrained presentation of Claude. Use direct, human language; urgency comes from real dates rather than pressure or gamification.

## Anti-references

Endless feeds, leaderboard motivation, loud control rooms, decorative metric dashboards, and unsupported promises of eligibility. Avoid a cold terminal aesthetic and a dense spreadsheet as the default home screen.

## Design Principles

- One next step, with a small choice of alternatives.
- Funding survivability before prestige.
- Evidence and uncertainty are visible beside a claim.
- User decisions are explicit and reversible.
- Private by default; a fictional demo remains useful to public visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA: keyboard navigation, semantic controls, visible focus, at least 4.5:1 body-text contrast, reduced motion, clear labels, and mobile layouts. Do not rely on color alone for status. These are implementation requirements, not a certification claim.
